General Anesthesia and Severe Maternal Morbidity Among Nulliparous Women: nuMoM2b Cohort

Poster

AANA Annual Congress 2026 · Warner, S., Bao, C., He, Y., Kandikonda, Y.S.R., Carlson, N.S., Wiltse Nicely, K.L., Chandler, R.D., & Manyam, R.

Overlap-weighted analysis of general anesthesia vs. neuraxial anesthesia and severe maternal morbidity in 7,798 nulliparous nuMoM2b deliveries. GA carried a 22.4 percentage-point higher adjusted SMM rate, consistent across sensitivity analyses; residual urgency confounding limits causal interpretation.
